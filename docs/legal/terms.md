## Acceptable use

Do not attempt to inspect, intercept, or reverse-engineer hidden game state in
order to gain an unfair advantage; do not disrupt other players' sessions; do not
use automated clients; do not use display names or emotes that are harassing,
hateful, or obscene. We may end a session that does any of these.
